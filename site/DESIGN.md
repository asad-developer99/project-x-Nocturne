# Design direction: Nocturne (Day 6)

**Brief:** Nocturne, a fictional luxury perfume house. One extrait in three glass colours (Noir, Ambre, Velours). Mood: night, smoke, warm light in the dark. Reel hook: *"A website you can almost smell."* All brand names invented, no real logos. Assets: made by the team in Flow (see "Assets" below).

**The idea in one line:** *a perfume told in light and smoke.* The page starts in total darkness; a single beam of warm light finds the bottle and, with it, the headline. Then one drop falls, splashes, and the scent rises out of the splash in three acts (top, heart, base). After that it is a real perfume house: three glass colours, sizes and prices, a scent finder, gifting, reviews, and a last look at the bottle on a rainy window ledge.

## Choices (codes from docs/DESIGN-MENU.md)

8 of 8 different from the last three sites (see the sites log).

| | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Look | **L1 Dark luxury, "chiaroscuro" version**: near-black smoke pages where light is the design. Every image lives in one warm beam from the top left; sections are lit, not filled. Lowercase italic serif, lots of black space, soft haze and film grain, hairlines that catch the light. No gold caps, no gold fills | A perfume is invisible; showing it as light and smoke in a dark room makes it feel almost smellable, which is the reel's hook |
| Palette | **Smoke & amber glass** (new). bg `#0d0b0a` (smoke black, neutral not brown) · surface `#171310` · text **warm ivory `#ece2d0`** · muted `#a39684` (6.8:1) · accent **amber glass `#e9a04c`** (9:1), used as *light*: glows, the beam, thin highlights, the liquid level. Buttons are **ivory** (solid ivory with smoke text, or ivory hairline), never amber fills. **Velours only:** burgundy `#6b1e2e` glow + `#b4475a` small highlight (never body text) | One accent, and it reads as glass held up to a lamp rather than metal. Ivory buttons keep it from turning into "gold on black" |
| Type pair | **T3 Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans**. Instrument Serif at huge sizes, mostly lowercase, with the *italic* for one word per heading ("night has *a scent*"). Instrument Sans for body, small spaced labels ("TOP · 0–15 MIN"), prices and buttons | A narrow, high-contrast serif with a beautiful italic: looks like a perfume label and gives big headings without shouting. Never used on an earlier site |
| Nav | **N6 Left vertical rail**: a slim rail on the left edge with "Nocturne" set vertically (outline letters that fill with amber as you read down the page, so the rail is also the progress bar), five small chapter marks (Light · Drop · Glass · Skin · Shop), and at the bottom a bag icon with a count. Top right, outside the rail: a small ivory "Shop" pill. Phone: rail becomes a slim top bar with the filling wordmark + a full-screen menu | Keeps the whole width free for the dark, wide images; the vertical name reads like the side of a perfume box |
| Hero | **The beam (new, "light finds it")**: full-screen darkness. Scrolling turns a beam of light on: the video scrubs from a black room to the bottle lit on a stone plinth, and the **same beam carries on across the headline**, lighting it letter by letter (charcoal → ivory with a warm rim) as it passes. Headline on the right half: "night has / *a scent.*" Small line below: "Nocturne — extrait de parfum". Then smoke drifts through the light | The light itself is the reveal: no shape opens, nothing grows to full screen. Text and video share one light source, so the reel reads as one cinematic shot |
| Section shape | **S3 soft curves, "meniscus" version**: sections meet on a gentle curved edge, like the surface of liquid in the drop video, with a thin amber light line along it. No arches, no torn edges | Ties every section to the drop: the page keeps "landing" on a liquid surface |
| Cards | **C4 Tall photo + text below, "blotter strip" version**: tall 3:4 photos with the label on a narrow paper tester strip (the blotter you spray in a shop: ivory strip, notched end, small spaced caps) hanging under the photo; prices on the strip | The blotter is the most recognisable object in a perfume shop; clients instantly picture a fragrance counter |
| Signature moment | **The Drop: scent rises out of the splash** (new). Pinned: one amber drop falls (video scrubs with scroll), lands centre and throws up a crown splash; **at the moment of impact three tall glass strips rise out of the liquid line**, one after another like scent lifting: **Top** (bergamot, pink pepper) → **Heart** (rose, smoke) → **Base** (amber resin, vanilla, oud). Each strip opens wide in turn (from the fragrance-mood strips pattern) with its note photo and the time it lasts on skin ("first 15 minutes · first hours · until morning"). The rebound jet and ripples play behind them. Supporting: **1. Glass colour sweep** (collection), **2. Mist on the wrist** | It shows how a perfume actually works (top, heart, base notes evaporating over time) in one scroll, and it is about the scent, not about a product spin or a colour switch |
| Loader | **"Concentration" counter**: on smoke black, the number rolls `0 → 22%` like an odometer under "Nocturne · extrait de parfum" (22% = extrait strength), a thin amber line fills under it, then the numbers blur into smoke and the darkness of the hero is already there. Fixed ~2.5 s, supports `&at=` | Shows the brand and a real perfume fact before the first beam; hands over into darkness so the hero's light is the first light |

**Motion feel: slow, smoky, exact.** Long, soft eases (`power2.inOut`, `expo.out` for light, 1.2–1.6 s), things arrive out of blur like scent, light sweeps rather than objects sliding. Nothing bounces. One thing moves at a time; the busiest moment is the drop.

Transitions: 0→2 the loader's digits blur into smoke over the already-dark hero · 2→3 **X1** statement slides up (on a meniscus edge) over the last 60vh of the pinned hero, which dims · 3→4 **X5** hard cut: the drop is already falling as the section's edge arrives · 4→5 **X3** the meniscus edge flattens as the ripples settle · 5→6 **X2** colour wash from smoke to the Noir blue-black glow · 6→7 **X1** wrist section slides over the last glass · 7→8 **X2** the mist hands its amber to the ledge light · 8→9 **X5** · 9→10 **X3** · 10→11 **X2** · 11→12 **X4** zoom-through: the rainy window arrives zoomed in (1.28×) and settles as it fills the screen (built in Round 3 instead of scaling the last review strip: cleaner on camera, no paper wall growing).

Drop timing (Round 5) is mapped to the video time of drop-amber.mp4, not frame numbers: the video fades in as the section lands, already falling · the fall 0.15 → 1.3 s at real speed (~1 s of record time) · impact → crown rising 1.3 → 2.3 s (1.2 s) · the crown at full height in slow motion 2.3 → 2.55 s (1.2 s, nothing in front of it, headline dimmed) · only then the notes rise from the liquid line while the crown falls back · the acts open and settle. Checked with stills every 0.25 s at 1440 and 390: the full crown alone on screen for ~1.5–2 s, no 1 s stretch that barely changes.

Smooth frame playback (Round 5): both videos use a blending frame player (`useBlendFramePlayer`, a site copy of the engine's: the next frame is drawn over the current one at the fractional opacity) on 48 fps frames, keep playing while their section scrolls away, and the crown / settled holds drift the video on slowly in record mode. Checked in-page: a new canvas image on every rendered frame during the hero and the drop (1440 and 390).

Never a frozen frame (Round 5, checked with ffmpeg freezedetect n=0.002 d=0.4 on the record runs): the loader's glow breathes while it preloads every hero and drop frame; the hero starts moving the instant the loader ends (no top hold, an early record stop, the shaft glides in); the crown lingers in slow motion instead of freezing; the main photos/videos breathe (1.00 → 1.03); during each record hold the held visual pushes in 1.00 → 1.02; the pinned collection pushes in slowly with the scroll; rain runs on the closing window. Holds: drop strips 0.4 s, each glass 0.5 s, closing 0.6 s (the crown plays in slow motion instead of holding). Record run: 36.9 s after the loader.

Details (Round 4, each with a hands-free play-once): magnetic ivory "Shop" pill and "Add to bag" · bag count bump on the rail when the collection's "Add to bag" plays · `data-cursor="Smell"` on note strips, "View" on bottles · blotter strips swing slightly on hover (and once by themselves) · amber underline draws on links · price flips 50 ml ↔ 100 ml on hover (once by itself in the collection).
